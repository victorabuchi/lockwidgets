Privacy Policy

Updated 17 October 2026

Notchlight doesn't collect your personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you see in Notchlight is sent to us.

What stays on your Mac

Notchlight reads these on your Mac to show them in its widgets and the island, and keeps them there:

  • Calendar events and reminders
  • The photos you choose for the Photos widget
  • Notifications, read from macOS's own notification database, which is why the island asks for Full Disk Access
  • The song playing in Music or Spotify, and Apple Music's lyrics from Music's own cache
  • How long you use each app, for the Recent Activity widget
  • Your layouts, widgets and settings

When Notchlight goes online

Some widgets need the internet. They contact these services directly, and each one sees your IP address, as with any website:

  • Weather: Open-Meteo, with your approximate location or the city you type.
  • Maps and place names: Apple Maps, for the Location and Earth widgets and the name of the place in Weather.
  • Stocks: Yahoo Finance, with the symbols you follow.
  • News: the feeds you pick, from BBC News, The Verge, The New York Times and Hacker News.
  • Artwork: album covers from Apple Music or Spotify.
  • Updates: once a day, Notchlight checks GitHub for a new version. Downloads come from GitHub too.

Each service's own privacy policy covers what it receives.

Permissions

Notchlight asks only for what your widgets use: Calendar, Reminders, Location, Photos, Automation to read the song in Music or Spotify, and Full Disk Access for notifications. You can turn any of them off in System Settings, under Privacy & Security.

Buying Notchlight

When you buy a licence, our payment provider handles the payment as the merchant of record, so your card details go to them, never to us. We receive your name, email address, country and licence key, and use them only for your licence and to help you.

This website

notchlight.com has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It remembers your Light, Dark or Auto choice in your own browser. Our host, Render, may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, to keep the site secure.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@notchlight.com
